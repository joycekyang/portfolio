## Project Overview

The core visual of this project features my standard poodle, Teddy, transformed from a casual photo into a high-fashion editorial cover for a playful publication titled **"DOGUE."**

1. **Complex Subject Masking:** Using the Select Subject tool, Refine Edge Brush, and precise clipping paths in Adobe Photoshop, I isolated Teddy’s intricate curly fur from the original background so the edges blended seamlessly against the new editorial backdrop.
2. **Editorial Color Grading & Dodge/Burn:** Applied Curves and Hue/Saturation adjustment layers to enrich the warm gold tones of the coat and make highlights pop, paired with subtle Dodging and Burning to accentuate facial structure and dimension.
3. **Accessory Compositing:** Composited a blue striped party hat and matching collar pom-poms onto the portrait with realistic contact shadows and soft blend modes, then exported with an alpha channel against a warm editorial taupe background for placement in Adobe InDesign.

## Process

I created a cohesive set of print assets comprising two main deliverables: a high-fidelity magazine cover and a companion subscription order card.

Using a clean serif masthead and a structured two-column editorial grid in Adobe InDesign, I balanced playful cover lines with precise typographic hierarchy for legibility and visual depth. This brand continuity extended directly to the companion subscription card, where custom form grids and print bleed settings mimicked an authentic publication insert.

## Results and Takeaway

The final deliverables demonstrate a seamless brand experience across cover photography and print layout. By isolating complex fur textures non-destructively in Photoshop and managing publication typography and bleeds in InDesign, this project bridged creative photo manipulation with print-ready technical standards.
