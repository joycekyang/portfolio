## Project Overview

As the host and lead organizer for the **TAP-SD Book Club** from September 2024 to July 2025, I led our bi-monthly literary series. The goal was to increase engagement among young professionals in San Diego by pairing contemporary literature (specifically highlighting Asian American and Taiwanese narratives) with a visually compelling brand identity. My role spanned from selecting titles and designing all promotional assets in Canva to managing the logistics of the live discussions.

To create a cohesive feel for the book club, I developed a consistent design system for our promotional flyers. Each selection (such as *Stay True*, *Green Island*, and *Interior Chinatown*) was given a distinct color palette and layout that reflected the book’s unique tone while maintaining a recognizable TAP-SD visual signature.

## Results and Takeaway

The cohesive Canva-led design strategy drove higher click-through and RSVP rates on social media, making the Book Club one of TAP-SD’s most visually recognizable and consistently attended community sub-groups.
