## Overview

To meet mandatory OSHA workplace safety standards (29 CFR 1910.157 and 1910.38), the organization needed an interactive asynchronous Fire Safety eLearning course to educate employees on fire prevention, emergency procedures, evacuation protocols, and proper fire extinguisher use. Collaborating with Subject Matter Expert John Malveaux, my task was to design and develop a scalable compliance course that increased awareness of fire safety practices, reinforced critical response steps, and formally assessed employee understanding.

## Impact

By handling end-to-end ADDIE development, UX design, and responsive authoring for 300+ learners in Bridge LMS, this course saved 92 hours annually in live compliance training. Success here also secured stakeholder buy-in for a pipeline of 34 future training modules.

Moving away from passive compliance toward active scenario practice made a real difference. Using Gagné's Nine Events kept complex steps clear without overloading learners, and the bold Neobrutalist design gave the interface strong scannability. Close teamwork with the SME and custom visual assets helped build a simulation that genuinely builds confidence under pressure.

## Design Style

* **Meeting the SME Vision:** During initial discovery, John was clear that he wanted to avoid typical corporate blues and grays, aiming instead for something visually bold and memorable that grabs attention right away.
* **Unambiguous Tactile Affordance:** The module relies on quick decisions and frequent interaction. Neobrutalism uses thick strokes and hard, sticker-style drop shadows to make buttons, hotspots, and clickable cards feel like physical objects you can press, eliminating guesswork about what is clickable.
* **Sharp Scannability and Hierarchy:** Using a high-contrast base with solid electric accents helps content containers stand out clearly, allowing learners to instantly distinguish between scenario storylines and actual controls.
* **Balancing Playfulness with Clean Function:** The design adds a modern, energetic feel without sacrificing accessibility or usability, creating an engaging learning space that feels purposeful rather than routine.

## Storyline Features Implemented

* **Interactive Branching Scenarios:** Placed learners directly into realistic emergency decision points to build situational judgment.
* **Click and Reveal Activities:** Broke down complex steps like the PASS technique into manageable, interactive actions.
* **Custom Variables and Badging:** Tracked learner progression and performance across slides. Once a learner completes a module, they earn a badge. Locked button states require learners to explore mandatory content before moving forward, and collecting all badges unlocks full progression.
* **Dynamic Button States:** Provided clear hover, selected, and visited feedback that matches the Neobrutalist design system.
* **Contextual Layer Feedback:** Triggered immediate, slide-layer pop-ups during end-of-module knowledge checks so learners understand the reasoning behind correct answers and remediation steps before proceeding.
</MediaSplit>

## LMS Data & Assessment Analysis

Beyond course launch, I regularly analyze learner analytics and item-level assessment reports in Bridge LMS to evaluate instructional effectiveness. By tracking completion rates and pinpointing which quiz questions learners repeatedly miss, I identify knowledge gaps, clarify ambiguous wording, and continuously refine course content to improve comprehension and retention.
