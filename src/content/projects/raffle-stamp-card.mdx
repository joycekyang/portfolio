## Project Overview

The primary goal of this initiative was to design a comprehensive engagement framework for the **2nd Annual TAP-SD Taiwan Festival**. The project aimed to transform the raffle from a simple prize drawing into a strategic tool for geospatial distribution, ensuring that attendees explored the full diversity of the 24 participating vendors while eliminating high-traffic operational bottlenecks.

Historically, the festival suffered from significant operational bottlenecks and imbalanced attendee distribution. A low barrier-to-entry for raffle participation allowed attendees to secure tickets with minimal effort, leading to severe congestion and long wait times at the central raffle booth while cultural and small-business vendors remained underutilized.

## Process

1. **Stakeholder Consultation & Scoping:** Conducted deep-dive consultations with the TAP-SD leadership team to establish core project parameters, identify all 24 participating vendors, and align visuals with the festival's established brand palette.
2. **Low-Fidelity Prototyping & Tiered Flow:** Developed initial mockups to visualize the placement of the stamp grid, instructions, and integrated QR code, testing the **6 / 12 / 24-stamp tiered reward system** for intuitive attendee comprehension.
3. **High-Fidelity Execution in Canva:** Created a clean vector linework aesthetic with custom vendor station blocks, balancing celebratory festival typography with crystal-clear legibility for raffle rules.
4. **Pre-Press & Print Production:** Conducted a thorough pre-press review verifying CMYK color accuracy, bleed margins, and serial number placement for printing on durable cardstock distributed to 2,000+ attendees.

## Results and Takeaway

The new raffle system was met with universal praise from both TAP-SD leadership and festival attendees. I personally oversaw operations at the central raffle booth, onboarding attendees and training volunteers on the new tiered flow.

Returning guests noted that the raffle flow was far more intuitive and streamlined than in previous years. By raising and structuring the participation thresholds (6, 12, and 24 stamps), we eliminated chronic booth congestion while driving enthusiastic foot traffic across all 24 vendor booths. This project reinforced that impactful graphic design is as much an operational tool as it is a visual one.
