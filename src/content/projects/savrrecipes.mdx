## Overview

**Savr Recipes** is a mobile app created for users to locate, save, and follow recipes and share reviews of dishes made at home. Recently, Savr had received negative feedback on recipes involving complex steps or advanced culinary techniques. Users who were excited to try a dish ended up frustrated because instructions weren't clear at the moment of execution. I ran a focused UX Design Sprint to prototype and test solutions within Savr’s existing brand system.

## User Research & Redesign Flow

* *“I know the basic definitions, like what minced garlic should look like. But a lot of times I see techniques that I am totally unclear on.”*
* *“I Google image search or YouTube, which kind of throws everything off, and means needing to drop what I am doing to use my phone.”*

1. **Improve clarity** of multi-step and advanced recipe directions.
2. **Improve delivery and timing** of steps and prep information.
3. **Guide users in-context** when learning new culinary techniques without leaving the recipe flow.
4. **Communicate common mistakes and quick fixes** proactively at critical cooking checkpoints.
