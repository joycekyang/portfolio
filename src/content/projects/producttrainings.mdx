## Problem

Traditional, synchronous onboarding and product training sessions place a burden on the training team, leading to inconsistent learner knowledge retention in the product line.

## Solution

Designed and developed a scalable, multi-part e-learning experience tailored for 500+ internal employees across diverse organizational functions. By integrating interactive scenario-based branching in Articulate Suite, modern visual assets via Canva, and rapid asset generation using Generative AI image pipelines, the learning experience transformed complex product architecture into modular, engaging digital modules that learners could complete asynchronously with high fidelity and zero direct trainer intervention.

1. **Script Writing & Storyboarding:** Translated dense technical product details into clear, role-relevant narratives and structured multi-part storyboards tailored for cross-departmental learners.
2. **Visual Design & Generative AI Assets:** Paired Canva layouts with custom Generative AI visuals to speed up UI and product illustration production.
3. **Interactive Prototype Development:** Built scenario-based branching interactions in Articulate Suite to enable self-paced, asynchronous mastery, eliminating 30 hours of repetitive live facilitation.
