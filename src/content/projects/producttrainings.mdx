## Problem

Traditional, synchronous onboarding and product training sessions place a burden on the training team, leading to inconsistent learner knowledge retention in the product line.

## Solution

Designed and developed a scalable, multi-part e-learning experience tailored for 500+ internal employees across diverse organizational functions. By integrating interactive scenario-based branching in Articulate Suite, modern visual assets via Canva, and rapid asset generation using Generative AI image pipelines, the learning experience transformed complex product architecture into modular, engaging digital modules that learners could complete asynchronously with high fidelity and zero direct trainer intervention.

1. **Script Writing & Storyboarding:** Translated dense technical product details into clear, role-relevant narratives and structured multi-part storyboards tailored for cross-departmental learners.
2. **Visual Design & Generative AI Assets:** Paired Canva layouts with custom Generative AI visuals to speed up UI and product illustration production.
3. **Interactive Prototype Development:** Built scenario-based branching interactions in Articulate Suite to enable self-paced, asynchronous mastery, eliminating 30 hours of repetitive live facilitation.

## End-to-End Development Phases

* **Phase 1: Analysis (Needs Analysis · September 2026):**
  * Collaborated with cross-functional subject matter experts across R&D, engineering, and manufacturing to scope core competencies and technical constraints.
  * Structured modular units around adult learning and multimedia design principles.
* **Phase 2: Design (Scriptwriting & Rapid Storyboarding · September – October 2026):**
  * Authored scenario-driven branching scripts mirroring real-world friction points for technical and non-technical staff.
  * Developed rapid low-to-high fidelity storyboards mapping UI interactions, decision branches, and knowledge checks.
* **Phase 3: Development (Visual Design & Generative AI Assets · October 2026):**
  * Established a cohesive visual design system using Canva for clean UI components, custom infographics, and iconography.
  * Built a Generative AI image workflow to produce brand-aligned visual assets and product mockups.
* **Phase 4: Prototyping & Iterative Testing:**
  * Developed functional interactive prototypes in the Articulate Suite with variables and conditional branching.
  * Completed thorough QA testing across every slide, verifying spelling, grammar, interactive triggers, and WCAG accessibility standards (POUR: Perceivable, Operable, Understandable, Robust).
* **Phase 5: Implementation & Impact:**
  * Deployed the final multi-part series across the company's LMS, tracking completion rates and interaction metrics.
  * Streamlined onboarding, reducing live facilitation time by 30 hours for the training team.
