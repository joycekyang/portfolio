## Overview

I worked with **WeConcile**, an early-stage, product-focused startup that developed a relationship therapy app designed to help couples strengthen their connection. I collaborated closely with the founders and CEO in weekly meetings alongside three other UX designers to design an **in-app community feature** aimed at increasing user engagement.

Key deliverables included competitive analysis (comparing invite-only vs. open community models across Slack and Reddit), user research, collaborative sketches, wireframes, typography/color system updates, iterative UI refinements, and an interactive high-fidelity Figma prototype.

## User Flow

* **In-App Community Flow:** Mapped the seamless user journey starting from the established product homepage into the new in-app community screen, channel feeds, and expanded thread discussions.
