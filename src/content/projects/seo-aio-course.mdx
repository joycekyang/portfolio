## Project Overview

Collaborating as one of five instructional designers, I worked closely with a marketing subject matter expert (SME) to structure and build this course through an initial discovery phase with the stakeholder, the business owner of a photography education business. Together, we translated domain expertise and business goals into clear, actionable learning modules designed for 200 photographers seeking sustainable organic visibility across both search engines and AI-driven discovery tools.

## Design Process

Our five-designer team aligned on goals, translated technical marketing concepts into photography-specific workflows, and structured modules for practical application.
