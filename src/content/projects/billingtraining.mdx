## Situation & Challenge

The Enterprise Enablement team spent over 20 hours weekly on repetitive live onboarding and retraining for the Esker Billing platform.

## Solution

Designed and deployed a scalable, self-paced learning and performance support solution to eliminate recurring live retraining, transition procedural training to an asynchronous model, and recapture ~80 hours/month of trainer time. Developed video demonstrations for both Vendor Registration and Approval flows for the accounts payable department.
