## Situation & Challenge

The Enterprise Enablement team spent over 20 hours weekly on repetitive live onboarding and retraining for the Esker Billing platform.

## Solution

Designed and deployed a scalable, self-paced learning and performance support solution to eliminate recurring live retraining, transition procedural training to an asynchronous model, and recapture ~80 hours/month of trainer time. Developed video demonstrations for both Vendor Registration and Approval flows for the accounts payable department.

## Design Process & Execution

1. **Analysis Phase:** Conducted a discovery call with the client to review and refine the script based on their standard training practices and real-world workflows.
2. **Design Phase:** Mapped and designed individual screen layouts to establish visual hierarchy, user flow, and optimal cognitive engagement before production.
3. **Development & Production:** Extracted precise screenshot images matching system aspect ratios and built out video demonstrations inside the Canva video editor. Generated professional narration using voice-over tools aligned to the script.
4. **Integration & Implementation:** Embedded the finalized video demonstrations into the Articulate Storyline file, incorporating interactive checks, and successfully deployed the module into the LMS for employee access.
